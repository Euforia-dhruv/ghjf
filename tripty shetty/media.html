In focus

Acts the firm defends under

  • 1985
    NDPS Act — Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. The NDPS Act prohibits any person from producing, manufacturing, cultivating, possessing, selling, purchasing, transporting, storing and/or consuming any narcotic drug or psychotropic substance — one of the strictest statutes in Indian criminal law, with bail thresholds unlike any other.
  • 1989
    SC/ST Act — Scheduled Castes and Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act. Enacted by Parliament to prevent atrocities against Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes — popularly known as the SC/ST Act, the POA Act, the Prevention of Atrocities Act, or simply the Atrocities Act.
