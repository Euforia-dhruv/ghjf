Practice areas

Fifteen criminal law specialities handled from a single office — bail applications to High Court writs.

01

Anticipatory Bail

When an arrest is feared, speed decides everything. Anticipatory bail applications are drafted and heard on urgent mentions across courts in Maharashtra — including matters secured during the Covid-19 lockdown through video conferencing when courts were closed.

Arrest and Remand

Representation at the moment that matters most — the produced-before-magistrate hearing. Challenges to police custody, argued remand oppositions and protection of rights from the first hour of detention.

Consult about arrest & remand →
03

Bail

Regular, interim and contested bail — with the strategy built around the charge sheet, the evidence and the forum. From session trials to special statutes, bail that survives scrutiny.

Consult about bail →
04

CDSCO (Drugs and Cosmetics Act)

Defence in prosecutions involving the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation — licensing issues, spurious drug allegations and regulatory proceedings under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.

Consult about CDSCO matters →
05

Cheating

Section 420 IPC cheating, criminal breach of trust and misrepresentation — for both the complainant seeking justice and the accused facing a fabricated FIR. Commercial disputes with a criminal shade handled end to end.

Cheque Bounce Cases

Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act — complaint filing, defence against dishonoured cheque prosecutions, and compoundable-matter negotiation to close cases fast.

Consult about cheque bounce →

COTPA Act

Defence under the Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products Act — prosecution for sale/display violations and compliance strategy for retailers and distributors.

Criminal Appeals, Revisions and Writ Petitions

Appellate work before the Bombay High Court and appellate forums — appeals against conviction, revisions of lower-court orders, and writ petitions for stay on FIRs, including quashing of false 498A cases.

Consult about appeals & writs →
09

Criminal Offences

Full-spectrum defence at trial under the IPC and CrPC — assault, hurt, wrongful restraint, murder and the whole range of offences, from chargesheet stage to final argument.

Consult about criminal offences →

Domestic Violence

498A dowry harassment, Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act matters and related matrimonial-criminal crossover — for complainants seeking protection and for families falsely accused.

Economic Offences

Financial crime defence — misappropriation, corruption-adjacent offences and offences affecting the economy, handled with the documentation discipline these cases demand.

Financial Frauds

Large-value fraud matters — including the ₹45 crore MHADA matter in which permanent bail was granted to all four accused officials by the Thane court. Banking, cheque and document-fraud intersections covered.

Consult about financial fraud →

SC/ST Act

The Scheduled Castes and Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 — bail, quashing and false-complaint defence under a statute with unique procedural rigour.

POCSO

Defence in the special courts under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act — where sensitivity and accuracy both matter. Bail secured in serious POCSO matters during the pandemic; father-son pair freed where charges were found linked to a property row.

Habeas Corpus
