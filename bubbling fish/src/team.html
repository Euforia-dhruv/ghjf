About the Founder

Nirala Rai / Founder

I am a mother of two girls who are keen swimmers. When my daughter was 6 years old, she was forced to jump at the deep end of the pool by her then teacher. This made her dislike swimming and she refused to get back into the water.

Heartbroken, I went to Partha, who suggested I do the AUSTSWIM course and teach my daughter myself. I completed my AUSTSWIM TSW course and started teaching both my daughters at Bangalore Club — they love the water now.

Watching this, club members wanted me to teach their kids as well. That encouraged me to take up swim teaching as a profession — I now teach at multiple centres, which gives me more satisfaction than my 10-year IT job that I eventually quit.

I am also an SSI-verified advanced diver and conduct ocean expedition trips to the Maldives where I introduce kids to the ocean.
